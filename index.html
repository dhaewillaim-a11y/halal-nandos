HALAL DINING AT NANDO’S

What is Halal Nando’s?

Halal Nando’s refers to specific UK branches that serve PERi-PERi chicken prepared according to Islamic dietary laws. The meat is sourced, processed and cooked under halal guidelines while preserving the signature flame-grilled taste. Find certified locations and full details: halal nandos – ensuring you dine confidently.

Not every Nando’s outlet is halal, but selected branches across major cities offer halal-certified chicken. The menu remains almost identical to regular Nando’s, with the same PERi-PERi sauces, spice levels, sides and desserts. This means Muslim customers can enjoy the authentic Nando’s experience without compromise.

FIND HALAL LOCATIONS

How to identify halal Nando’s branches

Official certification

Halal branches are clearly marked and certified by recognised bodies. Staff can confirm status.

Check online

Use official Nando’s site or halal branch guides to verify before visiting.

City availability

Major cities like London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds have multiple halal branches.

HALAL VS REGULAR

Key differences – what stays the same?

Same flame-grilled chicken

Halal branches use the same cooking technique, marinade and PERi-PERi flavour profile.

All spice levels available

Lemon & Herb, Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot – full range offered.

Identical sides & desserts

Chips, spicy rice, garlic bread, coleslaw, brownie – all unchanged.

The only difference is the halal certification of the chicken meat. Vegetarian items, sauces, sides and desserts are already suitable. This allows Muslim customers to enjoy the exact same Nando’s experience.

GROWING DEMAND

Why halal Nando’s is a trusted choice

The UK has seen a significant rise in demand for halal dining options. Nando’s halal branches provide a familiar, high-quality restaurant where families, students and professionals can dine without dietary concerns.

Consistency is another major factor – the same spicy, smoky flavour, the same fresh preparation, and the same casual atmosphere. Halal certification builds trust, while the ability to customise spice levels and sides makes every visit personal. For many, halal Nando’s is the go-to place for affordable, delicious flame-grilled meals.

DINING EXPERIENCE

What to expect when you visit

Halal Nando’s branches offer the same relaxed, modern and family-friendly environment. You can order at the counter, use digital kiosks, or order via app for takeaway. Food is cooked fresh after ordering, and bottomless drinks are available for dine-in customers. The staff are trained to answer any halal-related questions, so don’t hesitate to ask before ordering.

Most halal branches are located in busy urban centres, with ample seating and a vibrant atmosphere perfect for groups, casual lunches or dinner outings.
